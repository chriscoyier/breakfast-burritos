I like The Row because 1) it's rarely busy 2) the staff is always very friendly 3) I happen to be a social member at Tetherow so it's just nice to have a decent food option there. You don't have to be a member to eat at The Row, though.

The Row has a full kitchen and full breakfast menu, so like many other restaurants in this category, the breakfast burrito is a combination of stuff they happen to have around anyway wrapped in a tortilla. It's good though! I like that they don't just dump the home fries in there, they do some other kind of potato treatment unique to the burrito. The tortilla comes out toasted, a wonderful feature that only really works in the table-service setting. This trip, mine came out a smidge colder than I would have liked, but I've had this burrito a number of times and it's a perfectly good burrito worthy of your time.

Another classic is the watery salsa everywhere wants to give you with a breakfast burrito. The Row's is better than most, but should be even better as the salsa they give you at dinner with tortilla *chips* is excellent. Just get the Cholula.
