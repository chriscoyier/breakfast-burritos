Red Carpet is a... car wash. So, ya know, better than expected. I thought it was rice in there at first and didn't really see any eggs so assumed at first it was not really a breakfast burrito. But I think it was hash browns in the end and there were a light amount of eggs involved after all.

I wouldn't go out of your way for it but it's convenient while you're there. Or, ya know, it would be, except 5-star Don Gambino's is literally right across the street.
