They are evoking an Austin thing, which is welcome in this town. I have a feeling tacos are the way to go here (crazy, right? — just seems more Austin-like) but my burrito was darn good too! I went with green chile chorizo, but I'd definitely go back for the Al Pastor or "No Carne". It's skillfully made fresh on a flat top grill. That crisps up the tortilla which I typically like, but it makes to-go a little risky. Plan to sprint home or wait for nice weather to eat it there.

I went there on the very day that [The Bend Buzz](https://bendbuzz.beehiiv.com/) announced they were opening officially. It wasn't overly crowded there, tucked behind [Palate](https://www.palateacoffeebar.com/), but they were likely working out kinks. I ordered the sour cream and pickled pepper add-on and it was missing, as was the mango hot sauce they suggested. It also took ~25 minutes with no line. I'm cool with that, I just worry that when it's a little more busy it's going to be quite a wait.
