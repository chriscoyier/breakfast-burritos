This was just a random special, don't count on Bro Jo's for breakfast burritos in general. They aren't even open breakfast hours! I got this one at lunch because of course I couldn't resist it. It was good, but a reminder that it's not particularly difficult to slap together a decent breakfast burrito, particularly with a full kitchen. That's why I reserve my 5-star reviews for places that specialize or do something really above and beyond.

The server said they have been putting lettuce on them and talked me into asking for no-lettuce, which I thought was funny and a very good call.
