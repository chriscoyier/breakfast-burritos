Ancedotally, I've heard "it used to be better."

I believe it. Jackson's just isn't what it used to be. But I'm glad they are still here and it's a nice place to sit for breakfast.

It's a good burrito. It's hot off the griddle, it's got a little toast on the tortillia and I like that. It's full of good ingredients. But it's nothing special and it's a little pricey for what it is.
